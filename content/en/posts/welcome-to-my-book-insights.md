Welcome to this blog (yes, this term is still used, right?)! This is a space where I intend to share lessons, insights, and reflections that come from my experience reading technical books in Software Development, with a lot of help from LLMs, thank goodness!

First, some basic information about me: My name is Samuel, I was born in 1992 in [Fortaleza-CE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortaleza), the city where I currently live with my wife Deborah and my son Eduardo. I've been working in Software Development since 2012. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in early 2013 at [UFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Cear%C3%A1). Currently, I hold the position of Staff Software Engineer at [John Deere Bank](https://www.deere.com.br/pt/financiamento/banco-john-deere/).

Books have always been my main source of learning in this field, second only to day-to-day practice, of course. The goal here is to further enhance this learning by articulating the main ideas I extract from my readings and, perhaps, create opportunities for exchanging experiences and knowledge with readers.

I've always resisted the idea of writing a blog because... I don't like the activity of writing. I've always preferred reading (just as I prefer listening more than speaking). But with the advent of LLMs, writing has become more accessible. They say writing involves a bit of inspiration and a lot of perspiration - LLMs significantly reduce this perspiration part, allowing us to focus on inspiration. This makes the activity more enjoyable and efficient for the writer.

Moreover, whether we like it or not, writing is a fundamental - and, I dare say, indispensable - skill for those working in this field. As we advance in our careers, this need only increases and becomes more evident. Therefore, I also see this blog as an exercise to strengthen my writing muscles!

That's all for now. See you in another post, until then.
